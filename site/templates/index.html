Hello! I’m Jianye, a Ph.D. student at the Chair of Embedded Software (Informatik 11), RWTH Aachen University, supervised by Prof. Bassam Alrifaee.

I study learning- and optimization-based multi-agent decision-making for connected and automated vehicles. I am particularly interested in the interplay between reinforcement learning and control barrier functions.
